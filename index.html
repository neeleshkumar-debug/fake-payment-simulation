⚠️ NSS Cyber Education: How UPI QR Codes Work
A UPI QR code is just plain readable text like upi://pay?pa=name@bank&pn=Merchant. Fraudsters create fake screens simply by reading this text and putting it into fake demo apps!
Camera or gallery image will parse payee name (pn) & UPI ID (pa) locally.
Cyber Safety Alerts & Advisories
NSS Digital Financial Safety Guidelines
🚨 Modus Operandi: "Fake Payment Screen" Fraud
Scammers enter busy shops, pretend to scan the counter QR, and flash a green "Payment Successful" screen. They often say: "Sir, server is slow, money will come in 5 minutes" and walk away with goods.
✅ The 3-Point Merchant Defense Rule
1. Listen for Soundbox: Wait for the voice announcement.
2. Verify Bank SMS: Check your registered mobile phone for credit SMS.
3. Open Merchant App: Check your own transaction history before handing over items.
💡 National Cyber Crime Helpline
Dial 1930 immediately within the golden hour if you fall victim to financial cyber fraud, or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
